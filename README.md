Controls
---------
Use the WASD or arrow keys to change your snake's direction as it will move in the desired direction at all times. 
To increase your snake's length and therefore increase your score, run into the green dots (apples) and another will appear.
If you run into yourself or the wall the game will end and you will have to restart the program to play again.
